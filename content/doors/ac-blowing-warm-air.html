AC running but blowing warm air?

The short answer

Check 4 things: thermostat mode and fan setting, the filter, whether the outdoor unit is running, and ice on the copper line. Ice means switch cooling off and leave the fan on first. If those look normal, it is something you cannot confirm by eye: an outdoor electrical or start issue, a coil or airflow problem, or refrigerant. Those need measurements at the equipment.

Ten minutes, no tools

5 things to check now that could save you a service call

On their own, a thermostat reading and a photo of your filter are just notes. The LIVE walkthrough asks the follow-ups, structures what you saw, and hands you a packet you can send to whoever comes next.

  1. Your thermostat, exactly as it reads

    Mode, set point, room temperature, any code. Check the fan is on AUTO, not ON. A photo answers all of it.

  2. Your air filter

    If it looks dirty, show us the filter and its label. We read the size off it and name the replacement.

  3. Your outdoor unit, from a distance

    Is the top fan spinning? Is it buried in clippings? One wide photo from a few feet back. Do not open it.

  4. Your refrigerant lines

    Ice on the larger insulated pipe means switch cooling off, leave the fan running, let it thaw before anyone tests anything.

  5. Your vents, room by room

    Whole house or one end? Any registers closed or blocked? The answer splits the causes in two.

Before you start

What are people asking us at 9pm with a warm house

Why is my AC running but not cooling?
It is moving air but not moving heat. That happens when airflow is choked, when the fan is set to ON so it blows between cycles, when the outdoor unit has lost power, or when the refrigerant circuit cannot do its job. The first 3 you can check yourself in 10 minutes, and we will walk you through them.
Should I turn my AC off while it is blowing warm?
If there is ice on the lines, yes. Shut cooling off and leave the fan running. Running a frozen system can flood the compressor and turn a few-hundred-dollar repair into a few-thousand-dollar one. No ice and nothing smelling wrong, running it while you arrange a visit is usually fine.
Can I change my AC capacitor myself?
No. A run capacitor holds a charge after the power is off, and discharging one safely takes the right tool and the knowledge to use it. The part is cheap, which is exactly why this gets recommended online and why people get hurt. Identify the symptom, then hand it to somebody licensed.
How much should a repair on my AC cost?
Repair cost depends on the actual failure, equipment, market, access and the diagnostic work required. As verified quote and outcome records mature, the Repair Record is designed to publish comparable ranges with geography and sample size attached.
Is five companies about to call me?
No. Your request is never sold as a lead. If you choose to involve a provider it goes to one at a time, and nobody sees your details until you send them.
